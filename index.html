Values-based religious discovery

Find a faith tradition that fits the life you are trying to build.

This is not a truth detector or a personality quiz. It is a structured reflection tool that compares your values, spiritual instincts, and non-negotiables with broad religious and denominational patterns.

How this helps

  • Surfaces traditions that may fit your moral and spiritual orientation.
  • Explains why each match appeared instead of hiding behind a score.
  • Highlights tensions and questions to verify in a real local community.

Use this with care

No religion is reducible to a checklist, and local communities often vary from the broad patterns represented here. Treat the results as a grounded starting point for conversation, visits, and further study.

What this measures

Authority, ritual, inclusion, community structure, spirituality, moral reasoning, and the lived tone of belonging.

What this does not measure

Historical truth claims, the quality of a local congregation, or the depth of your own lived experience.
